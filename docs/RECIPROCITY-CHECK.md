# Reciprocity Hub: accuracy check (2026-10-21)

The hub's table has one row per state. This check covered ONE column: **does the state honor a Maryland resident's carry permit** (and, where an official page showed it, the Utah and Florida non-resident flags). It did not review the other columns (duty to inform, magazine limits, vehicle rules). No "last reviewed" date is shown in the hub for that reason.

## Method
Each state's official page (the links shown in the hub's "Source & review" box) was opened and read for what it says about Maryland. Nothing was changed unless the official page said so plainly.

## Not verified (no official statement found)
- **District of Columbia**: MPD's FAQ does not address recognition of other states' licenses. The hub's "does not recognize" is likely but not confirmed by an official page.
- **Hawaii, Oregon**: no official page found. **Rhode Island**: the Attorney General's packet describes a discretionary local permit and says nothing direct about recognition.
- **Kansas, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, Vermont**: official sites that block automated checks. Their links are not yet added to the hub until someone opens them.
- **Permitless ("constitutional carry") states**: their rules (age, residents versus non-residents) were not re-checked in this pass.
- **Washington and Utah non-resident permits**: Washington's page does not say whether a non-resident Utah permit qualifies.
