Maryland Legal & Licensing FAQ

Clear answers to keep you legally compliant and confident.

Do I need an HQL before or after I take the class?
You take the HQL training class *first*. Once you complete our course and receive your certificate, you will submit it along with your application and fingerprints to the Maryland State Police to obtain the license.
How long does the Wear and Carry application take to process with MSP?
By law, the Maryland State Police have up to 90 days to process your Wear and Carry application once it is accepted. Processing times can fluctuate based on application volume.
Do you provide fingerprints and photos, or where should I go?
We guide you exactly on what is needed. You will need to use a state-approved Livescan fingerprinting service. We provide recommendations for fast, local Livescan providers during your class.
What if I've never fired a gun before?
That's exactly why we're here! Our classes are built on a zero-intimidation foundation. We start with dry-fire safety protocols before you ever step onto a live range. You will leave feeling competent, safe, and empowered.
