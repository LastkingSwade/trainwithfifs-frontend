What to Bring & What to Expect

Important guidelines to ensure your class day is smooth, safe, and stress-free.

🔫 Handgun & Equipment

  • Do I need my own handgun? No! Rentals can be coordinated directly at Cindy's Hot Shots.
  • Bringing your own firearm? Must be unloaded and secured in a locked case or trunk during transit.
  • Holster (Wear & Carry): Sturdy OWB/IWB holster fitted for your firearm.

📦 Ammunition Protocol

  • Quantity: 50 to 100 rounds of factory brass target ammunition.
  • STRICT ZERO-AMMUNITION CLASSROOM MANDATE: Absolutely zero live ammunition is permitted in the classroom. Ammunition must remain locked in your vehicle trunk until live-fire qualification.

👓 Eye & Ear Protection

  • Wrap-Around Glasses: ANSI Z87.1 certified eye protection.
  • Hearing Protection: Earmuffs or foam plugs (electronic noise-canceling earmuffs recommended).

👕 Attire & Documentation

  • Clothing: Closed-toe shoes (no sandals), crew-neck shirt, and sturdy belt.
  • ID: Government-issued photo ID (Driver's license or Military ID).

About Lead Instructor Kai Wade

Founder & Chief Instructor, Future Initiative Firearm Services

Kai Wade is an NRA-Certified Firearms Instructor and a Maryland State Police (MSP) Certified Qualified Handgun Instructor (§ 5-101). He brings an analytical, patient, and modern approach to firearms education.

Rather than relying on intimidation or outdated military drill bravado, Chief Wade focuses on biomechanics, visual diagnostics, trigger press dynamics, and deep mastery of Maryland self-defense statutes. Every session emphasizes lawful conflict avoidance, situational awareness, and de-escalation first—with precision marksmanship as the bedrock of accountability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Maryland firearm permits, training requirements, and range days.

Future Initiative Firearm Services is built on a zero-intimidation, diagnostic coaching philosophy. Whether you have never touched a firearm before or are looking to refine your draw cadence, Instructor Kai Wade (Certified MSP Qualified Handgun Instructor § 5-101 and NRA Certified) tailors pacing to your individual comfort. You will gain genuine firearm safety proficiency and an understanding of Maryland self-defense law without judgment or drill-sergeant bravado.
Safety is absolute. Under no circumstances is live ammunition brought into the classroom. All live ammunition must remain secured in your vehicle trunk until Instructor Kai Wade conducts the live-fire evolution on the firing line.
No, absolutely not. Handgun rentals and safety gear can be coordinated directly at Cindy's Hot Shots. We recommend taking the class first to learn safe mechanics before purchasing.
A Handgun Qualification License (HQL) is required by Maryland law simply to purchase, rent, or receive a handgun. A Wear & Carry Permit (CCW) is the comprehensive certification required to legally carry a concealed handgun in public throughout Maryland. If you want both, our Combo class satisfies both mandates in one curriculum.
Our primary live-fire range is Cindy's Hot Shots at 115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD. Private and paired classes can also be scheduled at partner ranges across Anne Arundel, Baltimore, and Howard counties.
HQL applications are typically processed within 14 to 30 days. Wear & Carry (CCW) applications take approximately 45 to 90 days. We provide step-by-step follow-up guides inside your Student Portal to ensure your application has zero shortages in the MSP portal.
