Wall Trigger Press (Zero Deflection)

Focus strictly on front sight/reticle stability during the deliberate squeeze. Ensure firearm is triple-cleared and ammunition is stored in a separate room.

Repetitions: 25 Presses • 3 Sets

Immediate Action Drill: Tap, Rack, Assess

Simulate failure-to-fire scenario utilizing snap-caps. Seat the magazine vigorously with the palm heel, cycle slide with support hand over top, and re-acquire sights.

Repetitions: 15 Cycles

Live-Fire Qualification Packing List

  • Rigid Kydex or polymer holster completely covering trigger guard (No collapsible nylon).
  • ANSI Z87.1 rated wrap-around eye protection & electronic ear protection.
  • Minimum 50 rounds of factory brass ammunition (Cindy's Hot Shots range approved).
  • Stiffened EDC gun belt to support holster rigidity and draw stability.

Maryland Firearm Transport (CR § 4-203)

Non-permit holders must transport handguns unloaded, in an enclosed case or secure holster, directly between home, gunsmith, or range facility without discretionary stops. Loaded transport requires a valid Maryland Wear & Carry permit.

Federal FOPA Safe Passage (18 U.S.C. § 926A)

When passing through restrictive jurisdictions (DC, NJ, NY), firearms must be unloaded, locked in a dedicated container, and stored in the vehicle trunk or inaccessible compartment away from ammunition.
