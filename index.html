Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Maryland firearm permits, training requirements, and range days.

Why should I take a class with Future Initiative?

Future Initiative Firearm Services is built on a zero-intimidation, diagnostic coaching philosophy. Whether you have never touched a firearm before or are looking to refine your draw cadence, Instructor Kai Wade tailors pacing to your individual comfort without judgment or drill-sergeant bravado.

What is the 100% zero live-ammunition classroom policy?

Safety is absolute. Under no circumstances is live ammunition brought into the classroom. All live ammunition must remain secured in your vehicle trunk until Instructor Kai Wade conducts the live-fire evolution on the firing line.

Do I need to own a pistol before booking a class?

No, absolutely not. Handgun rentals and safety gear can be coordinated directly at Cindy's Hot Shots. We recommend taking the class first to learn safe mechanics before purchasing.

What is the difference between an HQL and a Wear & Carry Permit?

A Handgun Qualification License (HQL) is required by Maryland law simply to purchase, rent, or receive a handgun. A Wear & Carry Permit (CCW) is the comprehensive certification required to legally carry a concealed handgun in public throughout Maryland.

Where are training sessions and range qualifications conducted?

Our primary live-fire range is Cindy's Hot Shots at 115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD. Private and paired classes can also be scheduled at partner ranges across Anne Arundel, Baltimore, and Howard counties.

      🚗 Mid-Atlantic Highway Carry Corridors & FOPA 18 U.S.C. § 926A Safe Harbor

      I-95 South (MD → VA → NC → SC → GA → FL)

      100% Legal Carry Highway Corridor! Maryland Wear & Carry is honored in Virginia and North Carolina; South Carolina, Georgia, and Florida are Constitutional Carry. Remember: North Carolina requires immediate duty to inform upon officer approach.

      I-70 / I-68 Westbound (MD → PA → WV → OH → IN → IL → MO)

      Get a $20 PA Non-Resident LTCF to carry legally across the PA Turnpike. WV, OH, and IN are Constitutional Carry. When entering Illinois, out-of-state CCW permit holders are protected under Illinois in-vehicle safe harbor (must remain in car).

      I-95 North Warning (DE → PA → NJ → NY)

      Extreme Caution: Delaware does NOT honor MD resident permit (requires Utah/Florida non-res). New Jersey and New York have zero reciprocity with mandatory felony penalties. You must strictly store unloaded in locked trunk under FOPA § 926A.

      ✈️ Commercial Airline Flying with Firearms (49 CFR § 1540.111 & TSA Rules)

      Federal law permits airline passengers to transport unloaded firearms in checked baggage. Follow this mandatory 6-step checklist to ensure zero delays or legal penalties at airport counters:

      1. 100% Completely Unloaded

      Visually and physically inspect chamber and cylinder. Magazines must be completely empty unless securely enclosed in custom-molded case slots. Double check before leaving home.

      2. Rigid Hard-Sided Lockbox (Pry Test)

      Must be in a crush-resistant hard case (Pelican, Vaultek, Apache). Must not be pliable or priable by hand. Crucial Federal Rule: Use non-TSA padlocks only; federal regulations strictly require that ONLY the passenger retains the key or combination.

      3. Factory-Boxed Target Ammo

      Ammunition must be in original manufacturer cardboard or plastic grid packaging (under 11 lbs on all major domestic carriers). Loose ammo in bags is strictly forbidden.

      4. Declare at Main Ticket Counter

      Walk directly to the airline agent counter: "I have an unloaded firearm to declare in checked baggage." Sign the orange declaration card and place it inside your checked bag.

      5. Destination Laws Govern

      The moment you retrieve luggage at your arrival airport, the laws of that destination state apply to you immediately. Never fly with firearms to states where possession is prohibited.

      6. Emergency Flight Diversions (NY/NJ/MA)

      Critical Legal Protection: If diverted to NYC, Newark, or Boston, REFUSE physical custody of your checked bag at baggage claim. Demand the airline check it through to your final destination to maintain FOPA safe harbor.
