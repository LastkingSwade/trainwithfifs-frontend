Class Day & Range Preparation Guide

Complete packing lists and logistics for your classroom instruction and live-fire range qualification.

⚠ ZERO LIVE AMMUNITION IN THE CLASSROOM (STRICT MARYLAND PROTOCOL)

No Live Ammunition Allowed in Classroom: Absolutely no live ammunition is permitted in the classroom environment at any time. All ammo must remain locked in your vehicle trunk until the live-fire portion.
Firearm Transport: Handguns brought to class must be completely unloaded, stored in a locked transport case/box, and visually verified by Instructor Wade upon entry.

📖 Part 1: Classroom Day Preparation (Day 1 / Morning Session)

📝 Required Classroom Items

  • Government Photo ID: Valid driver's license or state ID required for enrollment verification.
  • Notepad & Pen: Essential for taking notes on Maryland statutes (SB 1, CR § 6-411) and de-escalation drills.
  • Personal Handgun (Unloaded): For dry-fire manipulation and holster fitment (strictly verified empty by instructor).
  • Comfort & Refreshments: Snacks and beverages (regular 10-minute focus breaks provided).

🧠 What We Cover in Class

  • Maryland Firearms Law: Sensitive locations, home defense / Castle Doctrine, and duty to retreat rules.
  • Use of Force: The 5 Legal Pillars of Self-Defense and the Threat Assessment Triad (AOI).
  • Safe Storage & Transport: Child access prevention (Jaelynn's Law) and 72-hour stolen firearm reporting.
  • Dry-Fire Diagnostics: Sight picture alignment, breath control, and trigger reset fundamentals.
🎯 Part 2: Range Day Preparation (Day 2 for 16-Hr Initial // Later That Day for 8-Hr Renewal)

📍 Range Schedule & Location

  • 16-Hour Initial Students: Live-fire qualification takes place on Day 2.
  • 8-Hour Renewal Students: Live-fire qualification takes place later that same afternoon.
  • Primary Facility: Cindy's Hot Shots (115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD).
  • Arrival: Arrive 10–15 minutes prior to range time to check in at the front counter.

🎯 Ammunition & Safety Gear

  • Ammunition Count: Standardized at 50–100 rounds of factory ammunition (locked in trunk until live fire).
  • Eye Protection: ANSI Z87.1 rated wrap-around safety glasses.
  • Ear Protection: Quality earmuffs (electronic earmuffs recommended to hear commands clearly).
  • Holster (Wear & Carry): Sturdy belt and rigid holster (OWB or IWB; no floppy nylon).

👕 Required Range Attire

  • Footwear: Closed-toe athletic shoes or boots (strictly no open-toe shoes or sandals).
  • Shirt: Crew-neck shirt or high collar to prevent hot brass from going down your shirt.
  • Headwear: Baseball cap strongly recommended to deflect ejected casings.
  • Weather / Comfort: Climate-controlled indoor range lanes at Cindy's Hot Shots.

Graduate Guide: Maryland State Police Portal

Step-by-step roadmap to complete your state application after passing your course.

Step 1: MSP MyLicense Portal

Access the official Maryland State Police licensing portal:

  • Navigate to emdsp.mdsp.org/emsp/
  • Create or sign in to your official user account.
  • Select HGP for Wear & Carry Permit or 77R for purchase licensing.

Step 2: LiveScan Fingerprints

Schedule fingerprinting with an authorized Maryland LiveScan provider:

  • Request a Maryland Handgun Permit (HGP) fingerprint background check.
  • Locate the 12-digit Tracking Control Number (TCN) on your receipt.
  • Accurately transcribe the TCN into your MSP portal application.

Step 3: Upload Certificate

Upload your training completion paperwork issued by Instructor Wade:

  • Upload a digital scan or clear photo of your signed MSP Training Certificate.
  • Include your scored qualification target sheet.
  • Ensure your 2x2 passport-style photo has a pure white/off-white background.

Step 4: 90-Day Renewal Window

Stay compliant and prevent permit lapses in the future:

  • Maryland Wear & Carry permits require renewal every 2–3 years.
  • Submit your renewal application at least 90 days before your permit expires.
  • Our 8-Hour Renewal Course is available year-round to fulfill state recertification mandates.
