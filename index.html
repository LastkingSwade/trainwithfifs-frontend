Do I need to own a handgun before taking the class?

No. Handgun rentals and target ammunition can be arranged directly on-site at Cindy's Hot Shots.

What is the zero live-ammunition classroom policy?

Under strict state safety mandates, absolutely zero live ammunition is allowed in the classroom. All ammo must remain secured in your vehicle trunk until live-fire evolution downrange.
