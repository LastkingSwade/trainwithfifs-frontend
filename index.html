Maryland State Police Licensing Portal Guide

Your official qualifications have been submitted. Follow these steps to complete your state permit filing.

1. MSP MyLicense Portal

  • Log in to emdsp.mdsp.org/emsp/
  • Start your HGP (Wear & Carry) or 77R (Purchase) application.
  • Monitor the portal's internal "Messages" tab for deficiency notices.

2. LiveScan Fingerprints

  • Obtain LiveScan fingerprints from an authorized Maryland provider.
  • Transcribe your 12-digit TCN number directly into your portal application.

Frequently Asked Questions

A Handgun Qualification License (HQL) is required by Maryland law simply to purchase, rent, or receive a handgun. A Wear & Carry Permit (CCW) is the comprehensive certification required to legally carry a concealed handgun in public throughout Maryland.
Our primary facility is Cindy's Hot Shots located at 115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD. However, private and small group sessions can also be adjusted or relocated to partner facilities across Maryland to accommodate your schedule.
