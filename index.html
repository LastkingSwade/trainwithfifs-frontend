🎯 Maryland Firearm Training Pathways

What to Bring & What to Expect

Important guidelines to ensure your class day is smooth, safe, and stress-free.

🔫 Handgun & Equipment

  • Do I need my own handgun? No! If you don't own a firearm, rental handguns and eye/ear protection can be coordinated directly at Cindy's Hot Shots.
  • Bringing your own firearm? Must be unloaded and secured in a locked case or lockbox during transit.
  • Holster (Wear & Carry): Sturdy OWB/IWB holster fitted for your firearm (no floppy nylon or cross-draw).

📦 Ammunition Protocol

  • Quantity: 50 to 100 rounds of factory brass target ammunition (no steel core or reloads).
  • STRICT ZERO-AMMUNITION CLASSROOM MANDATE: Absolutely zero live ammunition is permitted in the classroom at any time. Ammunition must remain locked in your vehicle trunk until live-fire qualification.

👓 Eye & Ear Protection

  • Wrap-Around Glasses: ANSI Z87.1 certified eye protection.
  • Hearing Protection: Earmuffs or foam plugs (electronic noise-canceling earmuffs recommended to hear commands clearly).

👕 Attire & Documentation

  • Clothing: Closed-toe shoes (no sandals), crew-neck or high-collar shirt (to prevent hot brass from falling down), and sturdy belt.
  • ID: Government-issued photo ID (Driver's license, Passport, or Military ID).

About Lead Instructor Kai Wade

Founder & Chief Instructor, Future Initiative Firearm Services

Kai Wade is an NRA-Certified Firearms Instructor and a Maryland State Police (MSP) Certified Qualified Handgun Instructor (§ 5-101). He brings an analytical, patient, and modern approach to firearms education.

Rather than relying on intimidation or outdated military drill bravado, Chief Wade focuses on biomechanics, visual diagnostics, trigger press dynamics, and deep mastery of Maryland self-defense statutes. Every session emphasizes lawful conflict avoidance, situational awareness, and de-escalation first—with precision marksmanship as the bedrock of accountability.
