Expanded Legal Reach

Train Once. Prepare for More Travel.

For Mid-Atlantic citizens who commute or travel along I-95 and I-70, crossing state lines without multi-state coverage can turn a lawful carrier into an accidental felon. We prepare you to navigate this legal landscape responsibly.

Maryland (Wear & Carry) Lead License

Your primary resident permit. Fulfills the 16-hour mandatory training requirement under Md. Public Safety Article § 5-306 and qualifies you for an HQL training exemption upon permit issuance.

Virginia (Non-Resident CHP) Multi-State

Our 16-hour curriculum satisfies Virginia's documented in-person handgun competency requirement, streamlining your non-resident concealed handgun permit application.

Florida (Non-Resident CWL) Multi-State

Florida's non-resident license is recognized across multiple travel states (including Delaware). Course qualification documentation fulfills Florida's live-fire training prerequisite.

Arizona (Non-Resident CWP) Multi-State

Arizona issues respected non-resident permits through the mail that recognize NRA-certified instructor training documentation, adding strong reciprocity depth.

Pennsylvania (Non-Resident LTCF) Guidance

Pennsylvania does not directly honor Maryland permits, but allows MD residents to obtain a discounted $20 Non-Resident LTCF at border county sheriff offices. We provide the complete step-by-step roadmap.

Mandatory Legal Notice: Firearms licensing, background checks, and reciprocity determinations are controlled strictly by each respective state government licensing authority. Train With FIFS provides state-approved education, practical qualification, and application documentation assistance; we do not issue permits or guarantee state approval. Reciprocity laws change frequently; always verify current statutory requirements before carrying in any jurisdiction.
