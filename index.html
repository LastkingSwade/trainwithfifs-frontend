What to Bring & What to Expect

Important guidelines to ensure your class day is smooth, safe, and stress-free.

🔫 Handgun & Equipment

  • Do I need my own handgun? No! Rentals can be coordinated directly at Cindy's Hot Shots.
  • Bringing your own firearm? Must be unloaded and secured in a locked case or trunk during transit.
  • Holster (Wear & Carry): Sturdy OWB/IWB holster fitted for your firearm.

📦 Ammunition Protocol

  • Quantity: 50 to 100 rounds of factory brass target ammunition.
  • STRICT ZERO-AMMUNITION CLASSROOM MANDATE: Absolutely zero live ammunition is permitted in the classroom. Ammunition must remain locked in your vehicle trunk until live-fire qualification.

👓 Eye & Ear Protection

  • Wrap-Around Glasses: ANSI Z87.1 certified eye protection.
  • Hearing Protection: Earmuffs or foam plugs (electronic noise-canceling earmuffs recommended).

👕 Attire & Documentation

  • Clothing: Closed-toe shoes (no sandals), crew-neck shirt, and sturdy belt.
  • ID: Government-issued photo ID (Driver's license or Military ID).

🚗 Car Travel & Interstate Highway Carry Corridors

When driving out of Maryland, your legal carry status changes at every state border. Use this travel guide alongside federal safe harbor protection (FOPA 18 U.S.C. § 926A):

I-95 South (MD ➔ FL Roadtrip)
Maryland (Permit Required) ➔ Virginia (Honored) ➔ North Carolina (Honored • Immediate Duty to Inform) ➔ South Carolina (Constitutional Carry) ➔ Georgia (Constitutional Carry) ➔ Florida (Constitutional Carry).
Status: 100% Legal Carry Highway Corridor for MD Permit Holders!
I-70 / I-68 West (MD ➔ Midwest)
Maryland ➔ Pennsylvania (Requires $20 PA Non-Resident LTCF; unloaded in trunk under FOPA otherwise) ➔ West Virginia (Constitutional) ➔ Ohio (Constitutional) ➔ Indiana (Constitutional) ➔ Illinois (⚠️ In-Vehicle Carry Safe Harbor only; do not carry outside car) ➔ Missouri (Constitutional).
I-95 North Warning (DE / PA / NJ / NY)
Delaware: Does NOT honor MD resident permit (Honors Utah & Florida Non-Resident permits).
New Jersey & New York: STRICT NO-CARRY ZONES. Must transport UNLOADED, separate ammo, locked in trunk under federal FOPA safe passage with no non-essential stops!

✈️ Flying with a Firearm: Mandatory TSA & Airline Protocols

Federal regulations (49 CFR § 1540.111) and travel procedures every student must follow when flying on commercial airlines with handguns.

01
Completely Unloaded
The firearm must be 100% unloaded. Chamber empty, magazine detached and empty, cylinder empty. Double visual and physical check.
02
Locked Hard-Sided Case
Must be secured in a rigid, hard-sided lockbox (Pelican, Nanuk, Vaultek, or sturdy factory case). The Pry Test: If the case can be pried open with fingers, TSA will reject it. Lock every available lock hole.
03
Non-TSA Locks Required
Crucial Federal Rule: Under 49 CFR § 1540.111, ONLY the passenger may retain the key or combination. Do NOT use TSA-accessible locks on your firearm case! Use standard key or combination padlocks that only you can open.
04
Ammunition Packaging
Ammunition must be packed in the manufacturer's original box or a dedicated plastic/fiber ammo carrier. Never loose in bags. Maximum 11 lbs (5 kg) per passenger. Ammunition can be inside the locked gun case or checked baggage.
05
Declare at Ticket Counter
Declare the firearm in person at the airline main check-in counter: "I have an unloaded firearm to declare in my checked baggage." Sign the declaration tag and place it inside your bag. Never take a firearm to a TSA passenger checkpoint!
06
Destination Laws Govern
The moment you retrieve your baggage at your destination airport, the laws of that destination state apply to you immediately. Only fly with firearms into states where your permit is honored or carry/possession is lawful.
⚠

CRITICAL LIFE-SAVING TIP: Emergency Flight Diversions (JFK, LGA, Newark, Boston)

If your flight to a legal carry state is diverted due to weather or mechanical issues into an unfriendly airport (such as New York JFK/LaGuardia, Newark NJ, or Boston MA), DO NOT TAKE PHYSICAL POSSESSION OF YOUR CHECKED LUGGAGE AT BAGGAGE CLAIM!
If the airline attempts to return your bags for an overnight stay, refuse the bag and demand the airline hold and check it through to your final destination. Taking physical possession of a firearm in NY/NJ—even during an unintended airline layover—terminates FOPA safe passage protection and can lead to immediate felony arrest by local airport police.

Maryland State Police Exemption

Maryland Designated Firearms Collector Status

What it does: Under Maryland Public Safety § 5-123 and COMAR 29.03.01.29, Maryland limits citizens to one regulated firearm purchase every 30 days. Approval as a Designated Collector completely waives this limit forever—allowing you to buy multiple handguns or receivers at the same time.

4 Steps to Become a Designated Collector:

  1. Download MSP Form 77R-3: A simple, 1-page affidavit from the Maryland State Police Licensing Division.
  2. Fill Out Your Details: State that you collect firearms for personal study, recreation, historical interest, or investment.
  3. Sign Before a Notary Public: The signature must be notarized (Lead Instructor Kai Wade can provide notarization or verification).
  4. Submit to MSP: Mail the notarized application to MSP Licensing Division in Pikesville, MD. There is $0 state filing fee!
