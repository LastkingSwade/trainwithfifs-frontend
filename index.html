Range Day Preparation & Packing Checklist

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the range with Instructor Wade.

⚠ CRITICAL SAFETY & TRANSPORT RULE (MARYLAND LAW)

Zero Live Ammunition in the Classroom: Absolutely no live ammunition is permitted in classroom instruction areas at any time. All ammunition must remain locked in your vehicle trunk until live-fire qualification.
Firearm Transport: Handguns brought to class must be completely unloaded, locked in a secure case or trunk, and visually inspected by Instructor Wade prior to range entry.

📍 Range Location & Arrival

  • Primary Range: Cindy's Hot Shots
  • Address: 115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD 21061
  • Arrival: Arrive 10–15 minutes early to check in at the front counter.
  • Accommodations: Sessions can be coordinated or relocated across Maryland based on prior scheduling.

🎯 Ammunition & Equipment

  • Ammunition Protocol: 50–100 rounds of factory target ammo (brass casing recommended; no steel-core).
  • Eye Protection: ANSI Z87.1 rated wrap-around safety glasses.
  • Ear Protection: Quality earmuffs (electronic earmuffs recommended to hear commands).
  • Holster (Wear & Carry): Sturdy belt and rigid OWB/IWB holster (no flimsy nylon or cross-draw).

👕 Required Range Attire

  • Footwear: Closed-toe athletic shoes or boots (strictly no sandals or open-toe shoes).
  • Shirt: Crew-neck shirt or high collar to protect against hot brass casings.
  • Headwear: Baseball cap strongly recommended to deflect brass.
  • Identification: Valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license or state ID).

Graduate Guide: Maryland State Police Portal

Step-by-step roadmap to complete your state application after passing your course.

Step 1: MSP MyLicense Portal

Access the official Maryland State Police licensing portal:

  • Navigate to emdsp.mdsp.org/emsp/
  • Create or sign in to your official user account.
  • Select HGP for Wear & Carry Permit or 77R for purchase licensing.

Step 2: LiveScan Fingerprints

Schedule fingerprinting with an authorized Maryland LiveScan provider:

  • Request a Maryland Handgun Permit (HGP) fingerprint background check.
  • Locate the 12-digit Tracking Control Number (TCN) on your receipt.
  • Accurately transcribe the TCN into your MSP portal application.

Step 3: Upload Certificate

Upload your training completion paperwork issued by Instructor Wade:

  • Upload a digital scan or clear photo of your signed MSP Training Certificate.
  • Include your scored qualification target sheet.
  • Ensure your 2x2 passport-style photo has a pure white/off-white background.

Step 4: 90-Day Renewal Window

Stay compliant and prevent permit lapses in the future:

  • Maryland Wear & Carry permits require renewal every 2–3 years.
  • Submit your renewal application at least 90 days before your permit expires.
  • Our 8-Hour Renewal Course is available year-round to fulfill state recertification mandates.
