Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers about Maryland requirements, range equipment, and scheduling.

No. You do not need to own a firearm prior to enrolling. If you are taking the Maryland HQL or an introductory session, firearm rental and equipment orientation can be coordinated directly at the range.
A Handgun Qualification License (HQL) is required by Maryland law simply to purchase, rent, or receive a handgun. A Wear & Carry Permit (CCW) is the comprehensive certification required to legally carry a concealed handgun in public throughout Maryland.
Our primary facility is Cindy's Hot Shots located at 115 Holsum Way, Glen Burnie, MD. However, private and small group sessions can also be adjusted or relocated to partner facilities across Maryland to accommodate your schedule.
Our ammunition protocol is standardized at 50 to 100 rounds of factory ammunition across all class tiers. This covers both diagnostic warmup strings and your official state practical qualification course of fire.
Please wear comfortable, range-appropriate attire: closed-toe shoes (no sandals), a crew-neck shirt, and a baseball cap (recommended). Bring wrap-around eye protection, hearing protection (electronic recommended), a government-issued photo ID, and your required ammunition.
We understand that schedules change. Simply reply to your booking confirmation email or contact Instructor Wade directly via phone/text with at least 48 hours notice to transfer your reservation to an alternate training date.
